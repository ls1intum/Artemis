After results are published, students get a window in which to inspect their assessed exam and object to it. The
window is bounded by **Begin of Student Review** and **End of Student Review** in the
[exam configuration](/instructor/exams/create-an-exam#exam-assessment-and-student-review); leave those blank and no
review takes place.

## Student Review

A student opens their **Summary** and sees a complaint button on every manually assessed exercise, with a text
field for their reasoning. What students see and do here is described in the
[student exam guide](/student/exams).

Automatically assessed exercises cannot be complained about, because there is no human judgement to dispute — with
one exception: once an assessor has reviewed the automatic result of a programming exercise by hand, that
assessment becomes open to complaint. This requires
[manual assessment](/instructor/exams/add-exercises#programming-exercises) to be enabled on the exercise.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.info}>

  A mistake in a quiz or programming exercise itself is not a complaint matter. Correct the exercise and re-trigger
  the evaluation, which updates every participant at once instead of one complaint at a time.

</Callout>

## Complaint Assessment

Complaints arrive in the **Assessment** dashboard, below the exercise submissions, and are handled like any other
assessment work. An assessor can accept or reject one; Artemis updates the result of an accepted complaint
immediately, and the new figures appear on the **Scores** page, from where they can be exported
([Exam Scores](/instructor/exams/grade-and-publish#exam-scores)).

The original assessor cannot answer a complaint about their own assessment — a second assessor has to. The
dashboard shows a progress bar for the complaint round, so you can see how many are still open.
