Conduction begins at **Visible from** and ends when the last student's working time is over. Students can open the
start page from the moment the exam is visible, and reach the exercises from **Start of working time**. Once a
student submits, their submissions are final.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.warning}>

  From the moment the exam is visible you can no longer change its configuration or the individual exams. Anything
  still outstanding — registrations, generated exams, prepared exercises — has to be settled before that date. See
  [Prepare and Test an Exam](/instructor/exams/prepare-and-test).

</Callout>

There is very little to do during conduction itself, which is the point of the checklist and the test run. What you
do need is a way to correct a mistake in an exercise while students are working on it.

## Updating an Exercise during the Exam

Programming, modeling, text and file upload exercises can be edited mid-exam. Open the exercise and choose **Edit**.
At the bottom of the edit page is a field for a notification text, which Artemis shows to every student currently
sitting the exam — so the correction reaches people who have already read the old wording.

<Image src={exerciseNotification} alt="Notification text field at the bottom of the exercise edit page" size={ImageSize.large} caption="A notification shown to students during the exam" />

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.info}>

  Write the notification as an instruction rather than a changelog. A student mid-exam needs to know what to do
  now, not what the exercise used to say.

</Callout>
