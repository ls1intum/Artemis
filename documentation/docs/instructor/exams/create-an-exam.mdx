## Accessing the Exam Management Page

- Log in to Artemis with your account credentials.
- Click on <img src={courseManagement} alt="Course Management" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', height: '2rem'}} />.
- Click on <img src={examsButton} alt="Exams" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', height: '2rem'}} /> for your course. It will open the *Exam Management Screen*.
- Here you have access to all the exams of your course. All aspects of the exam are managed from the management screen.
- You can create an exam by clicking on <img src={createNewExam} alt="Create New Exam" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', height: '2rem'}} />.

## Create and Configure Exam

### Exam Mode

- Artemis supports two exam modes. The conduction of normal exams and test exams.
- The normal exam mode is suitable for conducting end-of-semester exams. Students can view and work on the exam between the configured working time. Afterwards you can perform a manual or automated evaluation of the students' submissions. The results will be published on the specified date.
- Test exams provide you with the possibility to provide students with a practice opportunity for the end-of-semester exam. The main difference is that you choose a working window within which students can freely start the exam. Students then have the configured working time to complete the exam. After submitting, students immediately get the automated assessment for programming and quizzes. Manual correction of the submissions is not necessary.

### Create Exam

- When you click on <img src={createNewExam} alt="Create New Exam" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', height: '2rem'}} /> you are presented with the *Create Exam* view. Here you can set the basic information such as `title`, `examiner` etc.
- You can choose between the exam and test exam mode.
- The timeline of the exam is defined by the dates: `visible from`, `start of working time`, `end of working time`, `submission overview visible from`, `release date of results`, `begin of student review`, `end of student review`.
- The first three dates are mandatory when you create an exam. The rest can be set when required.
- The optional `submission overview visible from` date controls when students can see the summary (submission overview) of their submitted exam, including the exam questions, their own answers, and the PDF export. If you leave it empty, the summary is shown immediately after submission. Setting it to a later date is useful for exams written in several shifts, so that early submitters cannot download and pass on the exam content to students in later shifts. It must be after the `end of working time` and no later than the `release date of results`.
- The `grace period` defines the amount of time the students have at their disposal to hand in their exam after the `working time` is over. This is set to 3 minutes by default.
- Before the exam's assessment you can choose the `Number of correction rounds in exam`. If you want two tutors to assess a student's exam subsequently, set the number to two here. This enables the [second correction](/instructor/exams/assess-an-exam#assessment-with-second-correction-round).
- You can also define the `number of exercises` in the exam. You can leave this out initially, however it must be set before you can generate the [student exams](/instructor/exams/prepare-and-test#manage-student-exams). For more information, see [Exercise Groups](/instructor/exams/add-exercises#exercise-groups).
- Artemis will randomize the order of the exercises for each student if you activate `randomize order of exercise groups`.
- Finally, you can fill out the exam `start text` and `end text`. Artemis will present these texts to the students during the [exam conduction](/instructor/exams/conduct-an-exam#conduction), at the *Start-* and *End* page respectively.

### Import Exam

- Instead of creating a new exam, you can import an existing exam by clicking on <img src={importExam} alt="Import Exam" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', height: '2rem'}} /> from any of the courses you are an instructor in.
- Artemis displays a list of all available exams. To select one specific exam for the import, click on the <img src={importIndividualExam} alt="Import Individual Exam" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', height: '2rem'}} /> button.

<Image src={examImportSelection} alt="List with all exams available for import" size={ImageSize.large} caption="List with all exams available for import" />

- You are now presented with the *Import Exam* view. All information except for the dates are copied from the exam you selected for the import. You can find more information regarding this view at the section [create exam](#create-exam).
- Additionally, you can select or deselect exercises which are imported alongside the exam. You can find more information regarding the exercise import in the [section regarding the exercise group import](/instructor/exams/add-exercises#import-exercise-groups).
