Global search in the navigation bar lets everyone in your course find its exercises, lectures, lecture units, exams,
FAQs and channels, search inside the lecture slides and video transcripts, and ask Iris questions answered from the
course material. You do not have to set anything up for it. What a student can find follows the settings you already
use to release content, so this page explains which of them decide what appears. For what the search looks like to a
student, see [Global Search](/student/getting-started/global-search).

## What Students Find

A student finds only what they could also open in the course:

| Content | Visible to students |
|---------|---------------------|
| Exercises | Once released. Exam exercises once the exam has started |
| Lectures | Always, like on the course's lecture page |
| Lecture units | Once their release date has passed, or when they have none |
| Exams | Exams they are registered for, once the visible date has passed. Test exams once the visible date has passed |
| FAQs | Accepted FAQs only |
| Channels | Public and course-wide channels. The channel of an exercise or exam only once the exercise is released or the exam is visible |

Course messages and replies are not part of global search. Students search them in the course communication instead.

Course staff find more than students. Editors and instructors find every exercise and exam of the course, and
tutors find every exam once it is visible. Staff find every lecture unit and FAQ, released or not, and the public and
course-wide channels of unreleased exercises and exams, so you can check what an upcoming lecture contains before
students can see it.

Changes are picked up automatically. After you release, edit or delete something, the search reflects it shortly
afterwards; there is nothing to trigger or refresh.

## Slides and Video Transcripts

**Slides & Videos** searches the content of the lecture units' slides and video transcripts. Artemis processes that
content when you upload or change a unit, so a new unit becomes searchable once its processing has finished. For a
video, that includes transcribing it, which takes longer than processing slides.

The same release rules apply as for the lecture unit itself: students find a unit's slides and transcript only once
the unit is released, while staff find them right away. Pages you have hidden in an attachment are left out for
everyone, staff included, for as long as they are hidden. See
[Attachment Unit Management](/instructor/lectures/lectures#attachment-unit-management).

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.info}>

  Searching slides and videos uses AI models, so a student only sees these results after choosing to use AI in
  Artemis. You cannot make that choice for your students.

</Callout>

## Iris Answers and Your Iris Setting

When a student asks a question in the search, Iris answers it from the lecture content and the course information
described above, with each statement linked to its source. A student can then continue the answer in the course's
Iris chat.

Iris answers only use courses in which Iris is turned on. If you turn Iris off in the
[Iris settings](/instructor/communication-support/iris#turning-iris-on) of your course, global search no longer
answers from your course and no longer offers to continue in its chat. The course's slides and transcripts stay
searchable under **Slides & Videos**, because that is search and not an Iris feature. Turning Iris off does not
remove content that was already processed.

The answer applies the same visibility rules as the results list, so Iris never cites an unreleased exercise or a
hidden page to a student.
