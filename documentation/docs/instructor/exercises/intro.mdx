Artemis has five exercise types — [programming](/instructor/exercises/programming-exercise/),
[quiz](/instructor/exercises/quiz-exercise), [modeling](/instructor/exercises/modeling-exercise),
[text](/instructor/exercises/text-exercise) and [file upload](/instructor/exercises/file-upload-exercise) — and they
have the same skeleton. What differs is what a student hands in and how it is marked.

## What Every Exercise Has

- **Release Date** — when the exercise becomes visible. An exercise without one is visible immediately.
- **Start Date** — when students may begin working. Without one, they may begin as soon as it is released.
- **Due Date** — when submission closes. Assessment cannot start before it has passed.
- **Assessment Due Date** — when the assessments are published. Everything marked before it is released at once on
  that date, so no student sees their result before the others.
- **Points** and **Bonus Points** — what the exercise is worth, and **Include this exercise in the course score
  calculation**, which decides whether those points count toward the course score, count as bonus, or not at all.

## How Students Find an Exercise

**Exercises** in the course navigation opens the course's exercise list. The sidebar groups the exercises by where
they are in time, so a student sees what needs doing before what is finished:

- **Future** — released but not yet startable.
- **Due Soon** — open and due within three days.
- **Current** — open, and not due within three days.
- **Past** — the due date has passed.
- **No Date** — no dates set at all.

A group with more than five exercises is split into weekly subsections, so a long-running course stays navigable.
