A team exercise is one students hand in together: one participation, one submission and one result for the whole
team. Four of the exercise types support it —
[programming](/instructor/exercises/programming-exercise),
[modeling](/instructor/exercises/modeling-exercise),
[text](/instructor/exercises/text-exercise) and
[file upload](/instructor/exercises/file-upload-exercise).

## Create a Team Exercise

**Participation**, in the **Mode** section of the creation form, takes **Team** instead of **Individual**.
**Team Size** appears beside it and takes a **Min** and a **Max**; both are a recommendation that the dialog warns
about but lets you override when you actually build the teams.

<Image src={setTeamMode} alt="Mode section of the form with Participation set to Team" size={ImageSize.large} caption="Making an exercise a team exercise" />

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.danger}>

  **Participation** cannot be changed once the exercise exists. An exercise that should have been a team exercise
  has to be created again.

</Callout>

## Manage the Teams

**Teams** on the exercise's row in the course exercise list opens team management: every team with its tutor and
its members, and the three things you can do to the set as a whole.

<Image src={manageTeams} alt="Team management with two teams, their tutors and their members" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The exercise's teams" />

### Create Teams by Hand

**Create team** opens a dialog asking for the team's **Name**, its **Short Name** and its **Students**, searched
for by login or name. A **Tutor** can be given as well; a team without one is still a team, but the tutor is who
**Own teams** filters the list down to.

The short name identifies the team across the course, so a team of the same people on another exercise reached
through **Import teams** keeps it.

### Edit and Delete Teams

**Edit** on a team reopens the same dialog on it, and **Delete** removes it along with whatever it handed in.

<Image src={teamDialog} alt="Team dialog with a name, a short name, a tutor and three students" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="Editing a team" />

### Export Teams

**Export teams** writes the exercise's teams to a file that another exercise can import.

### Import Teams

**Import teams** takes teams either from such a file or straight from another exercise in the course. The dialog
colors each source team by what it would collide with — nothing, a short name already in use, or a student who is
already on another team here — and then asks for an **Import strategy**:

- **Purge existing** deletes every team in this exercise, and their participations with them, before importing.
- **Create only** imports the conflict-free teams and leaves everything already here alone.

<Image src={importStrategy} alt="Import teams dialog with the source teams and the import strategy" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="Importing teams from another exercise" />

## Collaboration

A team shares one editor: what one student writes, the others see. [Text](/instructor/exercises/text-exercise) and
[modeling](/instructor/exercises/modeling-exercise) exercises both work this way, and the text editor also lists the
team and marks who is in the exercise at that moment.

<Image src={liveTeamEditor} alt="Text editor with the team listed beside the shared answer" size={ImageSize.large} caption="A team writing together" />

## Team Overview

**Team**, on the exercise itself, opens the team's own page: who is on it, who its tutor is, and every exercise in
the course the team takes part in with what it has handed in for each. Students, tutors and instructors all see the
same page.

<Image src={teamOverviewPage} alt="Team page with the members and the exercises the team takes part in" size={ImageSize.large} caption="A team's page" />

## What Students See

Students on a team exercise share one participation and one result, and write together in the live editor — see
[Exercises](/student/exercises/intro) on the student pages.
