Your courses and exercises are in the **Artemis** view in the sidebar. It opens on the dashboard, and every other
page has a back link at the top.

## The Dashboard

**Current Workspace Exercise** names the exercise whose repository is open in VS Code; select it to open that
exercise. If no exercise is open, it says so, and **Choose** next to the heading opens the course list.

<Image src={dashboard} alt="The dashboard with the current workspace exercise, a recent course with its exercises, and the Tools & Settings list" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="The dashboard, with the exercise that is open in the editor at the top" />

**Recent Courses** lists up to three courses with up to four of their exercises each: the ones you opened last, and
until you have opened any, those with the most recently released exercises. **Show All** opens the full course list.

**Tools & Settings** holds **Browse Courses**, **Open Settings**, **Open Artemis in browser**,
**Submission Setup** (see [Working on an Exercise](/student/tools-reference/vscode-extension/work-on-an-exercise#submission-setup)),
**Bug Report**, which opens the extension's issue tracker, and **Logout from Artemis**.

## Finding a Course

**Browse Courses** opens **All Courses**, every course you are enrolled in. Search by title, semester or description,
narrow the list by **Type** and **Semester**, and change the order with **Sort by**. **Clear Filters** resets all three.

<Image src={courseList} alt="All Courses with the search field and filters, one active course and one archived course" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="The course list, with archived courses loaded" />

Courses whose end date has passed are archived and are not loaded at first. **Load Archived Courses** adds them in a
section of their own.

## A Course

Selecting a course shows its title, description and semester, and lists its exercises. Each row shows the exercise
type as an icon, the due date and the points; the exercise that is open in your workspace carries an **Open** badge.
Search the list or change its order with the dropdown beside the search field.

<Image src={courseDetail} alt="A course page with the Ask Iris card and three exercises, one of them marked Open" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="A course and its exercises" />

**Ask** on the **Ask Iris** card opens the Iris chat with this course as its topic; see
[Iris in VS Code](/student/tools-reference/vscode-extension/iris).

## An Exercise

An exercise page starts with the title, the points and the time left until the due date. Below come your
participation, the **Ask Iris** card and the problem statement under **Exercise Description**. The chevron beside the
title shows more details, such as the release date and whether the exercise is graded.

### Starting an Exercise

An exercise you have not started says **Not Participating Yet**. **Start Exercise** creates your repository in
Artemis, exactly as starting it in the browser does. **Open in browser** opens the same exercise in Artemis.

<Image src={exerciseNotStarted} alt="An exercise that has not been started, with the Start Exercise and Open in browser buttons" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="An exercise you have not started yet" />

After the due date, **Start Exercise** becomes **Practice**. It starts the exercise in practice mode, where your
work no longer counts towards your grade; see [Practice Mode](/student/exercises/practice-mode). Exams are not
available in the extension.

For text, modeling, quiz and file upload exercises, the page asks you to complete them in the browser and only
offers **Open in browser**.

### A Started Exercise

After you start, the participation block is headed **Repository Ready**. It shows the state of your local
repository, the **Submit** button and the result of your latest submission.

<Image src={exerciseDetail} alt="A started exercise with its latest result of 5 of 13 tests, the Ask Iris card and the problem statement" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="A started exercise, with the result of the latest submission" />

As in Artemis, the problem statement shows for each task how many of its tests pass. Selecting a task shows the
feedback for that task's tests only.

[Working on an Exercise](/student/tools-reference/vscode-extension/work-on-an-exercise) covers cloning, submitting
and reading the results.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.tip}>

  The icons at the top right of each page reload it from Artemis, open it in a larger editor tab, or open the
  extension's settings.
