A quiz is the one exercise type marked the moment it closes: every question has an answer a machine can check, so
you see your score without waiting for anybody to read your work. A quiz also runs against a clock — the working
time starts when the quiz starts, and when it runs out your answers are handed in as they are.

## Start the Quiz

Open the quiz from the course's exercise list. What you see next depends on how your instructor set it up.

Where each student takes the quiz whenever they like, **Start Batch** begins your run and the clock with it.

<Image src={currentQuizExercise} alt="Quiz before starting, with the duration and the Start Batch button" size={ImageSize.large} caption="Before you start" />

Where the whole cohort takes the quiz at once, there is nothing to press: the page waits, and turns into the
questions by itself the moment the quiz starts.

<Image src={pleaseWaitMessage} alt="Quiz waiting for the instructor to start it" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="Waiting for the quiz to start" />

## Answer the Questions

Each question carries what it is worth, and the question types differ in how you answer them:

- **Multiple choice** — tick the options you believe are right. Round buttons mean exactly one is correct; square
  ones mean any number may be.
- **Short answer** — type into the gaps in the text.
- **Drag and drop** — drag the items on the right onto the places in the picture they belong to.

<Image src={oneChoiceQuestion} alt="Multiple-choice question with two options ticked" size={ImageSize.large} caption="Answering a multiple-choice question" />

<Image src={dragAndDropView} alt="Drag-and-drop question with the items to place beside the picture" size={ImageSize.large} caption="Answering a drag-and-drop question" />

Artemis saves your answers as you go and the status bar at the bottom says when it last did. **Submit** hands the
quiz in early; if you do not, what you have answered is handed in when the working time runs out.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.warning}>

  The clock does not stop if you close the tab. Reopening the quiz brings your answers back, but the time you were
  away is gone.

</Callout>

## Your Result

The moment the quiz closes it is marked, and the page turns into your result: the score, and every question with
what you answered beside what was correct.

<Image src={finalQuiz} alt="Quiz result with the score and the first question's solution" size={ImageSize.large} caption="Your result" />

A drag-and-drop question shows your own placement first. **Show Sample Solution** replaces it with the correct one,
which is the quickest way to see what you put in the wrong place.

<Image src={solutionDragAndDrop} alt="Sample solution of a drag-and-drop question" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The sample solution" />
