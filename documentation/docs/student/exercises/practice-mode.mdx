# Practice Mode

Once the due date of an exercise has passed, you can keep working on it in **practice mode**. Practice submissions are never graded and never change your course score — they exist so you can finish a task you did not manage in time, try a different approach, or prepare for an exam.

Practice mode runs in a **separate participation** from your graded one. Your graded submission and its result stay untouched, and you can switch back and forth between the two at any time.

---

## Where Practice Mode Is Available

| Exercise type | Practice mode |
|---|---|
| Programming | Yes |
| Quiz | Yes |
| Text | Yes, if the exercise has AI feedback enabled |
| Modeling | Yes, if the exercise has AI feedback enabled |
| File Upload | No |

Two further limits apply everywhere:

- Practice mode is available for **course exercises only**, never inside an exam.
- It is not available for **team exercises**.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.info}>

  Text and modeling exercises are graded by people, so a practice submission would have nobody to assess it. They therefore offer practice mode only when your instructor enabled AI feedback for the exercise and your Artemis instance runs the Athena service — the AI takes the role of the assessor.

</Callout>

---

## Starting a Practice Participation

Open the exercise after its due date and click **Start practice** in the exercise header.

<Image src={startPractice} alt="Exercise header with the start practice action" size={ImageSize.large} caption="After the due date, the header offers Start practice" />

Artemis explains what practice mode does before it creates anything; confirm with **Practice**.

<Image src={practiceModePopover} alt="Practice mode confirmation" size={ImageSize.medium} caption="Confirming the start of a practice participation" />

For a **programming exercise**, Artemis sets up a second repository for you. If you already have a graded participation, you can choose what it should start from:

- **Practice with template repository** — start over from the exercise's starter code.
- **Practice with graded participation** — continue from the code you submitted before the deadline.

You work in the practice repository exactly as before: in the online editor or in your own IDE, and every push triggers a build with full feedback.

For a **quiz**, *Start practice* opens the quiz again with the same questions. You answer and submit as usual and get your result immediately, unrated.

---

## Switching Between Graded and Practice

When you have both a graded and a practice participation, the exercise header shows a toggle. Select a mode to see that participation's submission, result and feedback.

<Image src={participationModeToggle} alt="Graded and practice toggle in the exercise header" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The participation mode toggle switches between your graded and your practice work" />

If only one of the two exists, the header shows a read-only badge naming the mode you are in instead of a toggle.

---

## AI Feedback in Practice Mode

On exercises where your instructor enabled it, you can request **AI feedback** on a practice submission. The feedback appears like a normal result but is marked as automatic and is not graded.

This works for programming, text and modeling exercises. For text and modeling it is the same mechanism that makes practice mode possible there in the first place.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.warning}>

  AI feedback is generated automatically and can be wrong or incomplete. Treat it as a hint, not as an official assessment — it never replaces the grade from your graded participation.

</Callout>

---

## What Practice Mode Does Not Do

- It does **not** change your score. Results from practice submissions are unrated and excluded from the course score and from statistics.
- It does **not** extend the deadline. Your graded submission remains the one that was in place at the due date.
- It does **not** replace your graded participation. Switch back to *Graded* in the header to see what was assessed.
