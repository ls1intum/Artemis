## Find Your Exam

Sign in to Artemis and open your course. Choose **Exams** in the course sidebar, then select the exam. If your exam
appears at the top of the course list, you can open it directly from there.

Your instructor sets when the exam becomes visible. If you cannot find it when it should be available or Artemis
says you are not registered, contact your instructor or exam supervisor.

<Image src={accessExam} alt="An exam shown at the top of the course overview" size={ImageSize.large} caption="Reaching an exam from the course overview" />

## The Welcome Screen

Read the instructions on the welcome screen. Follow your instructor's rules about working independently,
communicating with others and using permitted resources.

Tick the checkbox to confirm that you have read the rules and will follow them. Type your full name exactly as
Artemis displays it beneath the field, then select **Start Exam**. Your typed name serves as your signature.

<Image src={welcomePage} alt="Exam welcome screen waiting for the exam to start" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The welcome screen before the exam begins" hideBorder={true} />

If you select **Start Exam** before the scheduled start, Artemis shows a countdown and opens the exercises when the
working time begins.

## Work Through the Exam

Select **Overview** in the exam sidebar to see every exercise, its title, type, points and submission status. Use
this list to find exercises that still need attention before you hand in.

<Image src={examOverview} alt="Exercise overview listing the exercises of an exam" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The exercises in your exam" />

Select an exercise in the sidebar to open it. A yellow save icon marks changes that still need to be saved or
submitted. The sidebar counts the exercises with saved submissions. You can collapse it for more space and still
see each exercise's status.

<Image src={examNavigationSidebar} alt="Exam navigation sidebar showing per-exercise status" size={ImageSize.small} caption="The navigation sidebar and its status icons" />

### Saving Your Work

For text, quiz and modeling exercises, Artemis attempts to save your changes every 30 seconds and when you switch
exercises. You can also select **Save exercise**.

For file upload exercises, select or drop a file and wait for the upload to succeed. For programming exercises,
select **Submit** in the browser editor or commit and push from your local editor. See
[Exercises in an Exam](/student/exams/exercises-in-an-exam) for the steps for each exercise type.

### Watching the Clock

The bar across the top shows your remaining working time. Allow time to check that your exercises are saved or
submitted before it runs out. If you think the timer is wrong, check for exam notifications and follow the
[recovery steps](/student/exams#two-rules-worth-following) if you need to reload.

<Image src={examBar} alt="Exam bar showing the remaining time and the hand in early button" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The exam bar" />

### Exam Notifications

If your instructor sends an announcement, changes the working time, or updates an exercise, check the message in
**Exam Notifications**. Open it from the megaphone icon next to the timer. Follow any instructions from your
instructor or exam supervisor.

## Hand In

When the working time ends, Artemis opens the end screen. You can also open it by selecting **Hand in Early**. If
you still have working time left and have yet to select **Finish**, choose **Back** to return to the exercises.
If Artemis asks you to wait for an attendance check before handing in early, contact your exam supervisor if you
need help.

1. Read the confirmation and tick the checkbox.
2. Type your full name as displayed under the field.
3. Select **Finish** and wait for the submission confirmation or the practice exam summary. If submission fails,
   check your connection, try again within the time shown and alert your exam supervisor if the problem persists.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.danger}>

  You must select **Finish** even if you have saved or submitted every exercise. If your instructor configured a
  grace period, use it to hand in after working time ends. Submit programming exercises and upload files during
  working time. An exam you do not hand in on time will not be graded.

</Callout>

Submission is final. When your summary is available, you can review your answers and save a copy for your records.
See [After the Exam](/student/exams/after-the-exam#your-summary).
