## Find Your Exam

An upcoming exam appears at the top of your course overview, and you can also reach it through the course and its
exams. It only becomes visible shortly before the working time starts, so do not worry if you cannot see it the day
before.

<Image src={accessExam} alt="An exam shown at the top of the course overview" size={ImageSize.large} caption="Reaching an exam from the course overview" />

<TumLiveVideo src="52546" title="Accessing Exams in Artemis" />

## The Welcome Screen

The welcome screen carries everything you need to know about the exam — read it rather than skimming it, because it
is where your instructor puts the rules that apply.

To continue you tick the checkbox confirming you have read the rules, type your full name, and choose **Start
Exam**. Your typed name is your signature; the name Artemis has on record for you is shown under the field, so
match it.

<Image src={welcomePage} alt="Exam welcome screen waiting for the exam to start" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The welcome screen before the exam begins" hideBorder={true} />

If the working time has already started, the exam opens immediately. If not, you wait — never more than five
minutes — and a message tells you how long is left.

## Work Through the Exam

The exercise overview lists every exercise in your exam with its title, type and points, and whether you have
submitted anything for it yet.

<Image src={examOverview} alt="Exercise overview listing the exercises of an exam" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The exercises in your exam" />

The navigation sidebar on the left moves you between exercises and shows the state of each one: not yet started,
changed but not saved, or saved and submitted. A counter at the top says how many of the total you have saved and
submitted, which is the number to watch. You can collapse the sidebar to give the exercise more room, and still see
each exercise's type and state.

<Image src={examNavigationSidebar} alt="Exam navigation sidebar showing per-exercise status" size={ImageSize.small} caption="The navigation sidebar and its status icons" />

### Saving Your Work

Text, quiz and modeling exercises have a **Save** button, and selecting any exercise in the sidebar also saves and
submits the one you are leaving. Between the two, your work is saved often without you thinking about it.

The other two types need an explicit action: a file upload exercise needs **Upload**, and a programming exercise
needs **Submit**.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.danger}>

  A programming exercise has no **Save**, and nothing auto-submits it. If you never choose **Submit**, your code is
  not graded — see [Exercises in an Exam](/student/exams/exercises-in-an-exam#programming-exercises).

</Callout>

### Watching the Clock

The bar across the top shows the time remaining and offers **Hand in Early**. The timer turns yellow when time is
nearly up.

<Image src={examBar} alt="Exam bar showing the remaining time and the hand in early button" size={ImageSize.large} caption="The exam bar" />

## Hand In

You reach the end screen either by choosing **Hand in Early** or automatically when the working time ends. If you
handed in early and changed your mind, **Continue** takes you back to the exercises.

As on the welcome screen, you confirm that you followed the rules and sign with your full name. The grace period
your instructor configured is added to the timer here, so you have a little time after the working time ends in
which to complete the hand-in.

<Callout variant={CalloutVariant.danger}>

  An exam you do not submit is not graded. Submitting is your responsibility and your instructors cannot do it for
  you afterwards.

</Callout>

Once you submit, nothing in any exercise can be changed.
